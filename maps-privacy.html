Privacy Policy

Local Leads & Maps Exporter to CSV — Chrome extension
In short
  • Reads only what's already public on the Google Maps search results page you're viewing.
  • Your exported CSV goes straight to your own device — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • One third-party service is involved: ExtensionPay, solely for the optional Pro purchase.

What this extension does

Local Leads & Maps Exporter to CSV reads publicly visible business listing data from pages you load at google.com/maps/search/* and lets you export it to a CSV file on your own device. That's its entire function.

What data it accesses

Google Maps search results page content. While you're on a Google Maps search results page, the extension reads the business listings rendered in the results list — business name, category, star rating, review count, address, phone number, website, and the coordinates embedded in each listing's map link. All of it is data Google Maps already shows publicly to anyone who loads that page. The extension does not access any Google account you're signed into, your device's location, nor anything outside google.com/maps/search/* pages.

Subscription status. The extension uses a third-party billing service, ExtensionPay, to check whether you've purchased Pro and to process payment if you choose to upgrade. ExtensionPay — via its processor, Stripe — handles anything you enter at checkout directly; this extension never receives or stores your payment details, only a paid/free status flag.

What data it stores

  • Your paid/free status is cached locally in chrome.storage.local so the extension doesn't need to check the server on every page load. This stays on your device.
  • Lead rows you export are written directly to a CSV file that downloads to your computer. The extension does not upload, transmit, or retain a copy of your exported data anywhere.

What this extension does not do

  • Track your browsing outside google.com/maps/search/
  • Collect analytics, telemetry, or usage statistics
  • Sell, share, or transmit your exported lead data to any third party
  • Require an account or sign-in to use the free tier
  • Access your device's location or Google account

Third parties

The only third-party service involved is ExtensionPay (billing and payment processing for the optional Pro upgrade). See ExtensionPay's privacy policy for how they handle payment information.
