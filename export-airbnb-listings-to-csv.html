How to export (download) Airbnb listings to CSV

Building a comp set for a short-term rental usually means copying Airbnb listings one by one. The free Airbnb Listing & Comp Exporter Chrome extension saves the listings from any Airbnb search to a spreadsheet in one click.

For short-term rental hosts, real estate investors, revenue managers and market researchers comparing listings in a neighborhood or city.

Add to Chrome, it's free

Free plan included · No sign-up · Runs only in your browser

Step by step

  1. Install Airbnb Listing & Comp Exporter from the Chrome Web Store. Free, no account.
  2. Search Airbnb for your area and dates.
  3. A panel appears showing how many listings it has recorded. Move the map to add more, or use Pause and Clear to keep only the area you want.
  4. Optional: keep only Superhost or Guest Favorite listings.
  5. Click Export and open the CSV in Excel or Google Sheets.

What's in the spreadsheet

  • Listing ID, title and details
  • Price per night and total price
  • Star rating and review count
  • Superhost and Guest Favorite status
  • Listing URL
  • Amenities, bedrooms, baths and guests (Pro Plus)

Files are standard CSV with UTF-8 encoding, so they open cleanly in Excel, Google Sheets and Numbers.

Questions

Can I download Airbnb listings?

Airbnb has no download option for search results. This extension saves them to a CSV file you can open in Excel or Google Sheets.

Can I export Airbnb search results to Excel?

Yes. The CSV opens directly in Excel or Google Sheets.

Is it free?

Yes, for up to 15 listings per search. Pro removes the limit.

Do I need an Airbnb account?

No. It reads the public search results you're viewing; no login needed.

Can I get amenities like pool or hot tub?

Yes, with Pro Plus: pool, hot tub, A/C, pet-friendly, self check-in, washer, free parking, EV charger, the full amenity list and bedroom/bath/guest counts.
